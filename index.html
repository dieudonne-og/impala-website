    Plumbing and hardware supplies in Kigali, Rwanda

    Impala Trading Ltd is a plumbing and hardware store in Nyarugenge, Kigali, open since 2008. We stock PPR pipes and fittings, HDPE and PVC fittings, Ganga and multilayer pipes, valves, taps and mixers, toilets and sanitary ware, shower sets, water heaters, gutters and electrical supplies from trusted brands such as IFAN, Era, Grohe and Milano. We also supply water pumps, solar energy equipment, generators, carpentry tools and safety equipment, and our contractor team builds water treatment plants and building works across Rwanda.
